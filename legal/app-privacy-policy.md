**Effective date:** October 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Suatu Rewards app collects, why, and what you can do about it. The program is run by Suatu ([SUATU LEGAL BUSINESS NAME], [MAILING ADDRESS]). The app is provided to Suatu by its operator ("we," "us"). We keep it simple: we only collect what the loyalty program needs, and **we never sell your information**.

Questions: **bmihaistudio@gmail.com**.

## 1. Information we collect

**When you create an account:**
- Phone number
- First and last name
- Email address
- Your password (stored only as a one-way hash — nobody can read it)
- That you confirmed you're 18 or older, and which terms you accepted and when
- Whether you want offers by email (optional)

**When you use the program:**
- Visits recorded by the cashier (date, time, store, points added)
- Rewards you redeem
- Basic technical information such as your IP address and device type, kept in security logs

**Before you have an account:** when a cashier enters your phone number at the counter, we store that number with the points earned, so you can claim them later. If no account is created and the number isn't used for **12 months**, we delete it.

## 2. How we use it

- To run the program: recognise you at the counter, add points, and let you redeem rewards
- To let you log in and reset a forgotten password (by email)
- To send offers by email, **only if you opted in**
- To keep the app secure and prevent fraud
- To meet legal obligations

We will **not** send you text messages. Your phone number is for identification and login only.

## 3. Sharing

We **never sell** your personal information and don't share it with advertisers. Suatu staff can see your name, a masked phone number (like ••• 0142) and your points. We use service providers to host the app and its database and to send email; they may only use your information to provide that service.

## 4. How long we keep it

As long as you have an account. When you delete your account in the app, we remove your name, phone number and email. We keep anonymous records of past visits and rewards for accounting.

## 5. Your choices and rights

In the app you can see and edit your details, download your data, turn off offer emails, and delete your account. You can also email **bmihaistudio@gmail.com**; we respond within 30 days.

**California residents:** you have the right to know, delete and correct your personal information, and not to be discriminated against for using these rights. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

## 6. Security

Encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords, access controls and login limits. No system is 100% secure; we'll tell you if a problem affects you, as required by law.

## 7. Children

The program is for adults 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

## 8. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the effective date and, for significant changes, tell you in the app before they apply.
