# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the operator of this website ("we," "us," "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit this website or join our waitlist. We keep it simple: we only collect what we need to tell you about our upcoming loyalty and specials app for local businesses, and we never sell your information.

If you have any questions, email us at **bmihaistudio@gmail.com**.

---

## 1. What this website is

This website describes a loyalty and specials app for independent local businesses that is **not yet launched**. Right now, the only thing you can do here is read about it and join the waitlist. This policy covers this website and the waitlist only. When the app launches, it will have its own privacy policy and terms.

## 2. Information we collect

**Information you give us when you join the waitlist:**
- Your name
- Your email address
- Your business name
- Your business type
- Your city
- The POS or register system you use
- Your phone number (optional)
- Your consent to receive launch updates

**Information collected automatically when you visit:**
- Basic technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device type, and the pages you visit, which our hosting provider records in standard server logs for security and reliability.
- Campaign information from the link you used to reach us (for example, "utm" tags showing you came from a social media post).
- If we turn on analytics, we use a privacy-friendly analytics tool that does not use advertising cookies or track you across other websites.

We do not use advertising cookies, and we do not knowingly collect sensitive information (such as financial account numbers, health information, or government ID numbers). Please don't include sensitive information in the form.

## 3. How we use your information

We use your information to:
- Send you updates about the launch and early access, if you agreed to receive them
- Contact you personally about becoming a founding business
- Understand what kinds of businesses are interested, so we can build the right product
- Keep this website secure and working properly
- Comply with legal obligations

We will **not** send you text messages unless you separately and explicitly agree to receive them. Giving us your phone number on the waitlist form is not consent to marketing texts.

## 4. How we share your information

We **never sell** your personal information, and we don't share it with advertisers.

We only share information with trusted service providers who help us run this website, and only as needed to provide their services to us. These may include:
- Website hosting (for example, [HOSTING PROVIDER])
- Database storage for the waitlist (for example, [DATABASE PROVIDER])
- Email delivery (for example, [EMAIL PROVIDER])

These providers may only use your information to provide services to us.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect our rights or the safety of others, or in connection with a business transfer (for example, if the project is merged into or acquired by a company). If that happens, this policy will continue to apply to your information.

## 5. Emails and unsubscribing

You can unsubscribe from our emails at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link in any email or by emailing **bmihaistudio@gmail.com**. We'll still be able to send you a message confirming your request.

## 6. How long we keep your information

We keep waitlist information until the app launches and for up to **12 months** after launch, so we can offer you early access and founding-member pricing. After that, or sooner if you ask, we delete it. Server logs are kept for a shorter period by our hosting provider.

## 7. Your choices and rights

You can ask us at any time to:
- Tell you what information we have about you
- Correct your information
- Delete your information
- Stop sending you emails

Just email **bmihaistudio@gmail.com**. We'll respond within 30 days. We may need to confirm your identity first (for example, by replying from the email address you signed up with).

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law:
- **California residents:** you have the right to know, delete, and correct your personal information, and to not be discriminated against for using these rights. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.
- **Residents of the European Union, European Economic Area, or United Kingdom:** we process your information based on your consent (for launch updates) and our legitimate interest in running and securing this website. You may withdraw consent at any time, object to processing, request a copy of your data, and complain to your local data protection authority.

We honor Global Privacy Control signals where required by law.

## 8. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information, such as encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access controls on the waitlist database. No website is 100% secure, but we work to keep your information safe and will let you know if a problem affects you as required by law.

## 9. Where your information is stored

Our service providers are based in the United States, so your information is stored and processed in the United States. If you visit from outside the United States, you understand that your information will be transferred there.

## 10. Children

This website is meant for business owners and adults. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has given us information, email us and we'll delete it.

## 11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the effective date at the top. If the changes are significant, we'll let waitlist members know by email.

## 12. Contact us

Questions, requests, or concerns? Email **bmihaistudio@gmail.com**.
