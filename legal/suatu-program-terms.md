## 1. Who can join

You must be at least 18 years old and live in the United States. One account per person and per phone number.

## 2. How you earn points

- You earn **5 points per visit** when a Suatu cashier enters your phone number at the counter.
- Points are added for purchases at Suatu, paid by cash or card.
- A cashier can add points to your phone number before you create an account. When you sign up with that number, those points move into your account.

## 3. Rewards

- **Free coffee: 100 points** (about 20 visits).
- To redeem, open the app, tap **Redeem**, and show the 6-digit code to the cashier within 5 minutes.
- Points above a reward's cost stay in your account (for example, 105 points − 100 = 5 points left).
- Rewards and their point costs are shown in the app and may change with notice (section 6).

## 4. What points are not

- Points have **no cash value**. They can't be sold, transferred to another person, or exchanged for cash.
- Points can't be used for fuel discounts unless the app says so for a specific offer.

## 5. Expiration

Points **do not expire** while the program runs.

## 6. Changes and ending the program

We may change these terms, the rewards or how points are earned, or end the program. We'll tell members in the app at least 30 days before a change that reduces the value of points, and before the program ends you'll have time to redeem the points you have.

## 7. Fraud and misuse

We may remove points or close an account if points were added by mistake, by fraud, or in a way that breaks these terms (for example, collecting points for other people's purchases).

## 8. Messages

We will **never send you text messages**. Your phone number is used only to identify you at the counter and to log in. Offers by email are sent only if you opted in, and every email has an unsubscribe link.
